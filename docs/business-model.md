# Business Model: Livestock Farm Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9212`
- ISCO-08: `9212`
- Occupation: Livestock Farm Labourers
- Social impact: worker-safety, animal-welfare, production-continuity

## Customer

- livestock farms / ranches / dairy and mixed-livestock operations
- independent livestock-farm crews / farm-labour cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- feeding/care-log/progress-record logging
- feed/farm-supplies procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to farm safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a livestock-handling-execution decision (e.g.
  approving a specific handling or restraint procedure), ever
- no direct finalization of a farm-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a farm cleared for safety), ever
- no override of a farm safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (animal-behavior hazard, outdoor-exposure
  hazard, farm-equipment hazard) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
